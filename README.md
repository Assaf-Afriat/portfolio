## Verification-Focused Background

My professional background is in control engineering and technical project delivery, where I worked from requirements and planning through implementation, testing, and final delivery.

That experience built a strong foundation in:
- structured engineering thinking
- debugging under real-world constraints
- system-level understanding
- disciplined execution and problem solving

Over time, I realized that the part I enjoy most is analyzing behavior deeply, thinking about corner cases, and building confidence that a design works correctly. That is what drew me toward Design Verification.

During my training, I built a full verification environment including RAL, SVA, and coverage, and I also worked on RTL design including a CDC asynchronous FIFO.

After the course, I continued developing my skills independently through several additional projects, including an AXI Stream RTL design project and a UVM-based ALU verification project. These projects helped me further strengthen both my RTL design skills and my verification-oriented mindset.
